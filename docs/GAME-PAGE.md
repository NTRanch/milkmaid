## What you do

You run a maid-service round. Hold to work the active method, and switch between four methods — pinch, clamps, pump suction, and cups — each with its own response curve on one body. Switch sides to address the other side. Pressure builds, arousal follows it upward, and when she lets down, the flow lands in whichever method is active — and the chart names it.

Beat the clock and reach the round's yield target. On the later rounds, yield alone is not enough: the win also requires let-downs across the circuit of methods, judged on the step itself. The chart screen archives every round of the sitting, and lifetime totals persist in the browser.

## Features

- Three rounds: Morning Round (2:30, 240.0 ml target), The Full Circuit (3:00, 420.0 ml, let-downs in at least 3 methods), and Made Service, In Full (3:30, 600.0 ml, let-downs in all 4 methods)
- Four switchable methods, each with its own response curve, plus two sides to address
- A full chart: every let-down grows capacity by 0.35 ml per addressed side, filed with the round's fluid timeline

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Work the active method (also confirms menus) | Hold canvas / SPACE |
| Switch to pinch / clamps / pump suction / cups | 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 |
| Address the other side | TAB or Q |
| Menu cursor · round picker | ↑ / ↓ |
| Confirm | ENTER |
| Pause / back | P or ESC |
